Who I Am

I'm a 21-year-old gap-year student working at the intersection of cybersecurity, privacy advocacy, and cultural technology. I believe the internet should be a space for genuine connection and knowledge-sharing, not algorithmic manipulation and data extraction.

My work centers on building alternatives: pitha.social, Bangladesh's first curated Mastodon instance rooted in Bengali culture; freelance security audits that help small organizations protect their digital presence; and teaching Bengali on iTalki as both income and cultural preservation.

I'm completing Google's IT Automation with Python certificate, hold their Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, and am preparing to study Computer Science in Argentina starting 2027. My long-term vision involves building tamper-proof knowledge infrastructure that serves humanity rather than shareholders.

Principles I Live By
